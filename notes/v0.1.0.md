**This is a beta.** Slouch Goblin works, but it has not been used by many
people on many Macs yet. Expect rough edges, and please report them.

Slouch Goblin 0.1.0 for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 13 Ventura or later.

## Install

Download the `.dmg`, open it, and drag Slouch Goblin to Applications, then open
it from there. Allow camera access when macOS asks — it cannot detect posture
without it.

The app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it
opens normally. No right-clicking, no Terminal commands, no security warnings.

## What it does

It lives in your menu bar and watches for forward lean through your camera.
After a short two-pose calibration, it monitors quietly. When you have been
hunching a while the screen takes on a soft green blur, and a goblin turns up
in the corner if you keep at it.

Your camera stays on your Mac. No video, image, or posture data ever leaves the
machine. The only network request is a daily check for a newer version, which
sends nothing about you and can be turned off from the menu.

## Known limitations

- Apple Silicon only. Intel Macs are not supported.
- Posture detection is calibrated to you, at your desk, in your lighting. A
  very different setup may need a recalibration.
- Not a medical device, and not ergonomic advice.

## Reporting problems

Open an issue. The menu has **Diagnostics… → Copy report**, which includes the
version and macOS build — pasting that into the issue helps a lot.

```bash
shasum -a 256 -c Slouch-Goblin-0.1.0-arm64.dmg.sha256
```
